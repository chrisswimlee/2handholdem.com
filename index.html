How to play

Five steps. Two shots at the dealer.

If you know Texas Hold’em, you already know 90% of the game. The new part is the split.

  1. 01

    The ante

    Place two equal bets in the Hand 1 and Hand 2 ante circles.

  2. 02

    The deal

    You get four hole cards face-down. The dealer gets three. The flop — three community cards — is dealt face-up.

  3. 03

    Arrange your hands

    The strategy phase: split your four hole cards into two separate two-card hands.

  4. 04

    Check or raise

    Check or raise 2× the ante on each hand, independently.

  5. 05

    The showdown

    Turn and river are revealed. Each of your hands plays against the dealer’s best five-card hand on its own.

About

Invented by a world champion.

2 Hand Hold’em was created by Wayne “D22-soso” Chiang — StarCraft: Brood War world champion, WSOP Talent Manager, and co-author of Betting On Yourself: Winning in Business, Poker, and AI.

The idea started in 2015 with a simple question: what if you could play two Hold’em hands at once against the same dealer board? A decade later it’s a patented, regulator-approved table game, a desktop game, and an iPhone app.
