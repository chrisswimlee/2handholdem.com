3. Official Rules of Play

By playing 2 Hand Holdem, you agree to abide by the official game rules:

  1. ANTE: The player must place 1 bet of equal value into each of the two "ANTE" betting circles to initiate the hand.
  2. DEAL: The player receives 4 hole cards face down. 3 community cards ("the flop") are dealt face up on the table. The dealer receives 3 hole cards face down.
  3. ARRANGE: The player arranges their 4 hole cards into 2 separate Hold'em hands containing 2 cards each. The player places 2 cards face down in the "Hand 1" box and the remaining 2 cards face down in the "Hand 2" box.
  4. RAISE: The player evaluates their positions and chooses to either Check (wagering no additional chips) or Raise 2x on each hand individually by placing additional chips equal to twice the Blind amount into the corresponding "Raise 2x" circle.
  5. SHOWDOWN: The 4th ("turn") and 5th ("river") community cards are dealt face up on the table. The dealer reveals their 3 hole cards and selects at most 2 of those 3 hole cards (combined with the 5 community cards) to form the dealer's single best 5-card poker hand. The dealer's hand is evaluated individually against Hand 1 (using the player's 2 Hand 1 cards + the board) and Hand 2 (using the player's 2 Hand 2 cards + the board). Winning bets are awarded based on traditional poker hand rankings against the dealer's hand.
