How to play

You play two poker hands against one dealer, sharing the same five community cards.

  1. Ante. Tap chips to set your bet, or hold one down to keep adding. The same amount goes on Hand 1 and Hand 2.
  2. Deal. You get four cards, the flop is dealt face up, and the dealer gets three cards face down. Your ante is locked from here.
  3. Split. Tap or drag your four cards into two hands of two. With a keyboard, press Enter on a card.
  4. Raise 2× (optional). Add twice your ante to either hand, both, or neither.
  5. Showdown. Deal brings the turn and river. The dealer plays its best five cards using at most two of its three hole cards. Each of your hands plays the dealer on its own: a win pays 1 to 1 on that hand’s whole bet, a tie pushes.

Table controls

  • Rebet & Deal plays the next hand with the same ante.
  • Auto deals the next hand by itself a moment after each showdown. Changing your bet or opening these rules pauses it.
  • After the showdown, tap Hand 1, Hand 2, or the dealer (or press Enter on it) to light up the five cards that made the hand.
  • Undo / Reset take back chips before the deal, or card moves while you split.
  • The line under your chips counts hands played and your net result since the last re-rack.
  • Out of chips? Re-rack gives you 1,000 practice chips.

Casino side bets

Live tables also offer optional side bets. They aren’t part of this practice table; paytables vary by casino.

  • Flounder 4pays on your four hole cards
  • Nessie 9pays on your four cards plus the board
